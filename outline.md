# Course Outline #
This is a preliminary outline of the course.

## Course Introduction ##
Week 1
1. Topics
1. Expectations
1. Schedule
1. Information Security
1. Book Discussion: Silver Blaze

## Historic Cryptography (Key Text: The Code Book and The Art of War) ##
Week 2-5
1. Caesar Shift Cipher (substitution)
1. Railfence Cipher (transposition)
1. Monoalphabetic Substitution Cipher
1. WWI Ciphers: The Zimmerman Telegram, ADVFX, and Playfair Ciphers
1. The Unbreakable Cipher: One-Time Pads
1. WWII Ciphers: The Enigma Machine and Navajo Code Talkers
1. Movie Fun: The Imitation Game

## The Psychology of Fear ##
Frankenstein

## Modern Cryptography ##
1984 and/or Brave New World
Week 9-12
1. How the Internet Works
   1. Protocols and Standards (phone call exercise)
   1. Internet Routing (postcard exercise)
   1. DNS, HTTP, and HTTPS

1. Encrypting with DES and AES
1. Cryptographic hashes and "random" number generators
1. Public Key Cryptography
1. Digital Certificates
1. Midterm?

Beyond Fear
Week 13-15
1. Examples of Security Exploits (HelloFresh, SQL Injection, Buffer Overflows)
