## Historic Cryptography ##
The purpose of the Historic Cryptography section is explore cryptography that can be processed "in your head" and to see how it has influenced world events throughout history. Although the ciphers here are simple, they use the same techniques as modern, complex, secure algorithms. The main differences involve changing from an alphabet based system to a binary based system and significantly increasing the key space.

The primary homework assignements are fun crypto puzzles and reading assignments. The reading assignments will be assessed with a quiz at the beginning of the next class. Students who did the reading should have no problem scoring high on the quizzes. Most readings will be assigned over the weekend, when students have more flexibility with their time.

Many of the classroom sessions will involve worksheets that explore the various cipher algorithms: including encryption, decryption, *and* cryptanalysis.

There will also be some class discussions regarding the reading assignments and students explaining how they solved the crypto puzzles.

The section will finish with a midterm exam that should only be *moderately* challenging for all students who have been keeping up with class.

**Key Text(s):** The Code Book by Simon Singh and The Art of War by Sun Tzu.
