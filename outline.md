# Course Outline #
This is a preliminary outline of the course.

## Course Introduction ##
The purpose of the Course Introduction is to provide an overview of the course and to set expectations. A lot of reading is required and students should come to class ready to engage with the texts and topic. Although this might be dreary if the subject matter was difficult or dry, most of our material will be interesting and engaging. So, to establish some precedent, the first night will include *three* Sherlock Holmes stories.  
Texts: Silver Blaze, The Speckled Band, and The Red-Headed League by sir Arthur Conan Doyle
1. Overview, Expectations, and Schedule
   1. 5 Pillars of Information Security
   1. Data at rest vs in transit
   1. Cryptographic Terms
   1. HW: Read the three Sherlock Holmes Stories
1. Quiz and Book Discussion
   1. What techniques does the adversary use to hide his crime? 
   1. How do the characters handle their fears?
   1. What sort of clues help Sherlock Holmes solve the case?
   1. What is the connection between crime mysteries and information security?
   1. HW: Getting to Know You Exercise
   1. HW: Read The Code Book Chapter 1 (pp 18-61)
