# The Playlist #

Every course needs a soundtrack.

For this short assignment, you will contribute one song to the unofficial course playlist. Choose a song that you think connects in some way to the themes of this course. Based on the limited amount that you know about the course thus far, your song might deal with ideas such as security, danger, fear, courage, trust, risk, anxiety, faith, hope, or responsibility.

The connection does not need to be obvious. A song does not need to use the words *fear* or *security* to belong on the playlist. An interesting personal connection is better than simply searching for "songs about fear."

## Your Response ##

Write one or two short paragraphs that:

- Identify the song and artist.
- States what the song is about.
- Describes how the song connects to one or more themes that seem relevant to this course.
- Explain why you think it belongs on the course playlist.

You may quote a short line from the song if it helps explain your choice, but the main purpose is your own explanation.

Remember that you have only limited knowledge of the course at this point. That is intentional. There is no secret list of correct songs. I am interested in what you currently associate with ideas such as fear, security, courage, trust, risk, and uncertainty.

## A Few Important Musical Disclaimers ##

You will not be required to sing your song in front of the class... probably.

You also do not need to worry that Prof. Tallman will lower your grade because he disapproves of your musical taste. Your grade will be based on the thoughtfulness of your explanation, not whether your song would make it onto his personal playlist: country, hip-hop, metal, worship music, classical music, Taylor Swift, movie soundtracks, obscure indie bands, Disney, and whatever else you listen to are all fair game.

If your greatest fear is that Prof. Tallman will silently judge your musical taste, then this assignment may already be helping us explore the themes of the course.

Keep the assignment short. Choose a song, make a thoughtful connection, and help us build the CSC-228 Course Playlist.
