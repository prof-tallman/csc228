# Security Comics #

Create a comic strip that explains a security concept. The comic strip should be just long enough to make a salient point about the internet, cryptography, or cybersecurity. You can make the comic funny, dramatic, romantic, or informative--the style of comic really doesn't matter--communicating something interesting about modern security is the important part.

The really funny example below is from XKCD. It sets the bar higher than what I expect any of us will be able to achieve.

![XKCD's Exploits of a Mom (aka Bobby Tables)](https://imgs.xkcd.com/comics/exploits_of_a_mom.png)

Here is a brief description of a potential comic, and one that might be more on our level. Hopefully it helps you get the creative juices flowing. Create three characters: a client computer, a web server, and a Certificate Authority. (Do they walk into a bar? I don't know). Have these characters "talk" through the basic steps of setting up a secure web session using public key cryptography. You could choose to have the session setup correctly or there could be an error like an invalid domain name or expired certificate.

### Memes ###

Your submission should primarily be a comic strip or short visual story. You may incorporate meme-style panels, images, or humor as part of the comic, but a single meme by itself does not satisfy the assignment.

## Requirements ##

Your comic must:

- Communicate at least one accurate security concept
- Be long enough for the idea or joke to make sense
- Be understandable to someone who has taken this class
- Be drawn by hand or created digitally

If the security concept is not obvious from the comic itself, include a short note explaining what the comic is illustrating.

Have fun with this assignment and please put some effort into it, but don't take yourself too seriously :)
