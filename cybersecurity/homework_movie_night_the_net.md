# Movie Night: The Net #

## Overview ##

For this assignment, watch the 1995 movie *The Net* staring Sandra Bullock. The movie was made when the Internet was just beginning to become part of everyday life. Some of its technology is dated, and some parts are exaggerated for Hollywood.

The purpose of this assignment is simply to watch the movie carefully and begin noticing some of the security problems that we will explore in the next few class sessions. Don't worry about deciding whether every technical detail is realistic. Instead, pay attention to the larger questions the movie raises about computers, security, identity, and trust.

## Questions ##

After watching the movie, answer the following questions. Keep your answers brief, usually just a few sentences each.

1. What was your favorite or most memorable scene?

2. What are the attackers trying to accomplish?

3. Find one example where a computer allows someone to do something that they probably should not be allowed to do.

4. Angela knows who she is: Angela Bennett. Why does she have so much trouble proving that to other people and computer systems?

5. What is one question about computers or security that the movie left you wondering about?

## Submission ##

Submit your answers to the five questions above. You do not need to write an essay or summarize the movie.
